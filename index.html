BMW Motorrad
Brand Campaign · Women's Day

Rider Is Neutral

IgnitionRiding is culturally coded as a "man's game," even though the word "rider" carries no gender. That bias sidelines thousands of women riders.
ApproachRan a social experiment asking the audience to describe a "good rider" — then confronted them with their own gendered assumptions.
ExecutionFeatured real stories from 11 women riders on the bias they've faced, reframing gender as irrelevant to riding ability.

Brand Campaign · April Fools'

A Fool, Indeed

IgnitionSociety calls anyone who defies convention a "fool" — the mad scientist, the crazy artist, the rider who chases sunsets instead of comfort.
ApproachFlipped the usual April Fools' prank format. Instead of a fake product, reframed "fool" as a badge of honour for BMW Motorrad riders.
ExecutionA three-part visual series — facing the breeze over a hot bath, the desert over a club, the wild over a stream — each closing on "a fool, indeed."
